### contradicts-spec (proven violation)

#### Finding 1 (F-66dd52ee) — Header canonical encoding omits the `bedrock_version` byte and writes `slot` before `parent_block`, so the header is 296 bytes, every derived size is short by one byte and the signature preimage is not the spec's 297-byte header — **confirmed**, medium severity, high confidence

The specification defines the header wire format as `Header = Version ParentBlock Slot BodyRoot ProofOfLeadership` (297 bytes), with `bedrock_version` a 1-byte field "fixed to 0x01", and derives from it `SignedHeader` = 361 bytes, `UncleHeaders` ≤ 1,445 bytes, `Proposal` = 364..18,192 bytes and Payload Formatting's `Max_Body_Length = 18192`. Construction step 5 signs "the 297 bytes of the header alone" in that canonical encoding. The code's `Header` has no version field at all, and its hand-written encoder lays the fields out as `slot, parent_block, body_root, proof_of_leadership` for a canonical size of 296. Everything built on it inherits the shift: `SignedHeader` is 360 bytes, `UncleHeaders` at most 1,441, `Proposal` 363..18,187, and the Blend payload bound `MAX_PAYLOAD_BODY_SIZE` (outside the unit) becomes 18,187 rather than 18,192. `Header::sign` and `verify_header_signature` therefore sign and verify a 296-byte slot-first preimage, and header-validation rule 1 ("the bedrock version") has no field to read.

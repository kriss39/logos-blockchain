### missing-requirement (proven violation, demonstrated by a failing test)

#### Finding 1 (F-dd99b3f2) — CLAIM_POW_REWARD has no proof type, so rule 6's ZkSignature by `public_key` over `mantle_txhash` is never checked — **confirmed**, high severity, high confidence

The specification's Proof paragraph requires the claim's `op_proofs` entry to be "A ZkSignature by the secret key corresponding to `public_key`, over the transaction's `mantle_txhash`", and validation rule 6 states "The claim must be signed by the key the reward is paid to": `assert ZkSignature_verify(mantle_txhash, claim_proof, [claim.public_key])`. The stated purpose is that "a solution cannot be found by searching over public keys directly".

The code has no counterpart. `impl ProvableOperation for ClaimPowRewardOp` declares `type Proof = NoOpProof`, so `OpProof::None` is the only proof a claim can carry, and `verify` checks rules 1 to 5 and returns `Ok(None)` with neither a signature check nor a deferred ZkSig verification. Every other ZkSignature-proved operation (Transfer, SDP active and withdraw, channel deposit) does defer a `ZkSig` over the transaction hash. Two consequences follow, both on the consensus path. First, an unsigned claim whose `public_key` is a bare field element found by searching public keys directly is accepted, its reward note is minted and `pow_reward_pool` is debited; nothing ties the claim to the owner of `public_key` or to the carrying transaction, so a pending claim can also be lifted into someone else's transaction. Second, a transaction built like the specification's worked example, with `op_proofs=[ZkSignature(reward_sk, mantle_txhash(tx)), transfer.prove(reward_sk)]`, is rejected before validation as an op-proof mismatch, and the wire format cannot carry the signature since the codec counts 0 proof bytes for a claim. A spec-conforming implementation and this node therefore disagree on every block that contains a claim, and claiming is enabled in the shipped testnet deployment template (`rate_num: 1`).

What the finding does not establish: it shows a reward note minted to an unsigned claim and the pool debited, within the ledger's own admission path; it does not demonstrate a loss of third-party funds, and it does not quantify how often an unsigned claimant finds a ticket below `difficulty_reward`. The gas constant of 1 is noted as predating any signature but is not part of this finding (see G2).

- Locations: `core/src/mantle/ops/pow.rs:286` (`type Proof = NoOpProof`), `core/src/mantle/ops/pow.rs:313-326` (`verify` runs rules 1-5 and returns `Ok(None)`), `core/src/mantle/ops/op.rs:285` (`does_proof_match` expects `OpProof::None` for a claim); *ctx* `core/src/mantle/ops/signed_op.rs:397-404` (an `OpProof::ZkSig` on a claim becomes `OpProofMismatch`), `core/src/mantle/transactions/codec.rs:61` (0 proof bytes for a claim), `ledger/src/lib.rs:887-905` (claim execution: nullifier recorded, note minted, pool decremented).
